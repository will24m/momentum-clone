# Privacy Policy Draft

## Momentum Todo Workspace

Momentum Todo Workspace collects only the data needed to store and sync your todo list.

### What the extension stores

- task text
- task completion state
- task ordering metadata
- display preferences such as theme and completed-section behavior
- account identifier data needed for sync when you sign in

### What the extension does not collect

- browsing history
- page contents from websites you visit
- keystrokes outside the extension
- advertising identifiers
- third-party tracking data

### Local storage

The extension stores workspace data locally in the browser so your list can appear immediately when you open a new tab.

### Cloud sync

If you enable sync, your todo data is stored in the configured backend for your account so it can appear on your other devices.

### Data sharing

Your data is not sold. It is only sent to the storage/auth providers required to operate the sync feature.
